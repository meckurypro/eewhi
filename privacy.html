Privacy Policy.

This policy explains how Eewhi Technical Solutions handles information when you visit this website or contact us.

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. About this policy

Eewhi Technical Solutions ("Eewhi", "we", "us" or "our") is an IT, software and technology solutions business. This policy applies to information collected through this website and information you choose to provide when contacting us.

2. Information we may receive

This website does not provide user accounts, visitor profiles, payment processing or a backend contact form. If you contact us by email, telephone or WhatsApp, we may receive information you choose to share, such as your name, contact details, organisation, project requirements and the contents of your message.

Our hosting provider or infrastructure services may process limited technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device information and request logs, for security, delivery and troubleshooting.

3. How we use information

We may use information you provide to respond to enquiries, discuss or deliver requested services, prepare proposals, provide customer support, maintain business records, protect our systems and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies and third-party services

This website does not intentionally use advertising cookies or behavioural analytics. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and may display photographs hosted by Unsplash. Those providers may receive technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser requests their resources. Their own privacy policies govern their processing.

Links to WhatsApp and email applications are provided for convenience. When you follow those links, the relevant third-party service's terms and privacy policy apply.

5. Sharing and service providers

We may share information with trusted service providers where reasonably necessary to operate our business, respond to you or deliver an agreed service. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect legal rights, safety and security. We do not disclose enquiry information for unrelated marketing by third parties.

6. Retention and security

We retain correspondence and business information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including customer service, project delivery, record-keeping and legal requirements. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information we hold about you, and to object to or request limits on certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Children

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
